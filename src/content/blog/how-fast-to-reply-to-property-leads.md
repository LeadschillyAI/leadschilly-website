A buyer fills in an enquiry on a property portal at 10:30 pm. Your team sees it at 10 am the next day. By then the buyer has spoken to two other brokers, and one of them has already offered a site visit for Saturday.

Nothing went wrong with your marketing. The lead was real, and you paid for it. You simply replied too late.

## What the research says about reply speed

The best-known study on this is from Harvard Business Review. Researchers looked at about 1.25 million sales leads received by 42 companies. Firms that tried to contact a lead within an hour of the enquiry were nearly **7 times more likely** to have a meaningful conversation with the decision maker than firms that waited even one hour longer. They were more than **60 times more likely** than firms that waited a day or more.

The same research audited how fast companies actually responded to a web enquiry. Only 37% replied within an hour, 24% took more than a day, and 23% never replied at all.

The study is from the US and is not specific to real estate. But the pattern is easy to recognise in any broker's office: the warmest moment of a lead is the minute after they enquire.

## Why property leads go cold even faster

Property buyers rarely enquire about just one project. They browse portals and social ads in the evening, send several enquiries in one sitting, and then talk to whoever replies first with something useful.

That means the first broker to respond does not just get a conversation. They often get to set the shortlist, suggest the projects and book the first site visit.

## Where replies get stuck

Most teams are not slow because people are lazy. Replies get stuck in the gaps between systems and shifts:

- **Portal emails sit in an inbox** until someone checks it and copies the details across.
- **Leads arrive after hours.** Evenings, Sundays and festival weekends are when buyers browse most, and when offices are closed.
- **Distribution takes time.** A lead waits for a manager to assign it, or goes to a rep who is out on a site visit.
- **One call, then nothing.** A rep calls once, the buyer doesn't pick up, and the lead is never touched again.

## A 5-step playbook to reply within minutes

### 1. Send the first reply on WhatsApp, straight away

WhatsApp is where Indian buyers already are, and a message can go out at 11 pm without waking anyone up. The first message should arrive while the buyer is still looking at their phone.

### 2. Ask the four questions that decide fit

Budget, location, configuration and timeline. Ask them one or two at a time, not as a form. Here is a simple first message:

> Hi Rohit, thanks for your interest in the 3 BHK. To share the right options, what budget range are you looking at, and when are you planning to move?

### 3. Offer two specific visit slots

"Would you like to visit?" invites a "maybe later". "Saturday at 11 am or Sunday at 4 pm?" invites a choice. Once the buyer picks one, confirm it with the location pin and send a reminder the day before.

### 4. Follow up on a schedule, not from memory

Decide your follow-up rhythm once and apply it to every lead: the same day, the next day, day 3 and day 7. Each message should carry something useful, such as a price sheet, a floor plan or a new visit slot.

### 5. Measure your first-reply time every week

Track one number: how long it takes from enquiry to first reply, for every source and every rep. What gets measured gets fixed. If it is measured in hours, the steps above will pay for themselves quickly.
