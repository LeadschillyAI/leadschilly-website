Refund and Cancellation Policy

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how payments, cancellations and refunds work for Leadschilly. Customers in India are billed by Leadschilly Technologies Private Limited. Customers outside India are billed by Leadschilly LLC, USA.

1. Free 30-day pilot and free trial

The pilot, and the 14-day trial for businesses that sign up themselves, are free. We don’t charge anything during it, so there is nothing to refund. At the end of it you choose whether to start a paid plan. Nothing renews into a paid plan unless you ask for it.

2. Monthly plans

  • Plans are billed monthly, in advance. You pay each month by the invoice or payment link we send, or by automatic payment if you choose to set it up. If you use automatic payment, your bank or payment app tells you before each charge, and you can cancel the automatic payment at any time.
  • A monthly payment is not refundable once it has been charged, including for unused days or users.
  • You can cancel at any time. Your plan stays active until the end of the month you have paid for, and you won’t be charged again.

3. How to cancel

Email admin@leadschilly.com from the account owner’s email address, or message us on WhatsApp at 080 6217 8859. We confirm the cancellation by email.

4. When we do refund

  • Billing mistakes: if you are charged twice, charged the wrong amount, or charged after cancelling, we refund the wrong charge in full.
  • Tell us within 30 days of the charge. Refunds go back to the original payment method within 7 to 10 working days of our confirmation.
  • For customers in India, refunds include the GST paid on the refunded amount. We issue a GST credit note for it.

5. Charges from other companies

WhatsApp message charges are billed to you directly by Meta, and call charges by your calling provider. Leadschilly doesn’t collect these, so refunds for them are handled by those providers under their own policies.

6. Service delivery

Leadschilly is software delivered online. Nothing is shipped. We activate your plan once your payment is confirmed, usually within one working day.
