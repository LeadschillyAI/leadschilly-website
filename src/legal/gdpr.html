GDPR: information for people in the EU and UK

Last updated: 25 October 2026

This page applies to people in the European Economic Area (EEA), the United Kingdom and Switzerland, under the General Data Protection Regulation (“GDPR”) and the UK GDPR. It adds to our Privacy Policy, which lists the data we collect and the providers we use.

1. Who is responsible

  • For our website, our users and our own sales contacts, the controller is Leadschilly Technologies Private Limited, WorkFlo, Ranka Junction, Vijinapura Village, KR Puram Hobli, Bengaluru 560016, India. For customers outside India, Leadschilly LLC, 30 N Gould St, Ste #3569, Sheridan, Wyoming 82801, USA, is the controller of their account data, and Leadschilly Technologies Private Limited processes it on the LLC’s behalf.
  • For the leads and contacts a business manages in Leadschilly, that business is the controller and we are its processor. A data processing agreement, including the EU Standard Contractual Clauses where needed, is available for customers who need one: contact admin@leadschilly.com.

Leadschilly is focused on India and does not currently market its Services in the EU or UK. If we start offering the Services to businesses in the EU or UK, we will appoint representatives there where Article 27 of the GDPR and the UK GDPR requires, and list them here.

2. Why we use your data (legal bases)

  • Contract: to provide the Services to the business you work for, and to set up and run your account.
  • Legitimate interests: to keep the Services secure, prevent spam and abuse, provide support, and reply when you contact us. We balance these interests against your rights.
  • Consent: for analytics cookies on our website, and for any marketing that needs consent. You can withdraw it at any time.
  • Legal obligation: to keep invoices and respond to lawful requests.

3. Cookies on leadschilly.com

We use Google Analytics to see which pages and buttons are used. For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, analytics cookies are switched off by default and no analytics cookie is set without consent. Google may still receive basic, cookieless signals that don’t identify you. The web app (my.leadschilly.com) uses only the cookies needed to keep you signed in.

4. Where your data goes

Our main database is in India (Mumbai). Some providers in our Privacy Policy, such as Cloudflare, Google, Meta and our AI providers, may process data in other countries, including the United States. When personal data from the EEA, UK or Switzerland is transferred to a country without an adequacy decision, we use the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses (with the UK Addendum where needed) or another lawful transfer mechanism.

5. How long we keep it

Only as long as needed for the purposes above. Account data is deleted within 90 days after an account closes (within 30 days if the owner asks us to), and website analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Section 10 of the Privacy Policy has the full list.

6. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your personal data and get a copy;
  • correct inaccurate data;
  • have your data erased;
  • restrict how we use it;
  • receive it in a portable format;
  • object to processing based on legitimate interests, and to direct marketing at any time;
  • withdraw consent at any time; and
  • not be subject to decisions with legal or similarly significant effects based solely on automated processing. Leadschilly’s lead scores help sales teams prioritise and are not used to make such decisions about you.

Email admin@leadschilly.com with the subject “GDPR request”. We reply within one month. If your data belongs to a business that uses Leadschilly, we pass your request to that business and help it respond.

7. Complaints

You can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work, or, in the UK, to the Information Commissioner’s Office. We would appreciate the chance to resolve it first: write to admin@leadschilly.com.
