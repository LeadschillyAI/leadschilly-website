Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026

This policy explains how Leadschilly Technologies Private Limited (“Leadschilly”, “we”, “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use our website leadschilly.com, the Leadschilly web application, the Leadschilly mobile app, and the booking pages and forms we host for our customers (together, the “Services”). It is written with reference to India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the rules under them, and the Meta Platform Terms. Customers outside India sign their contract with Leadschilly LLC, USA. For them, Leadschilly LLC is responsible for their account data and Leadschilly Technologies Private Limited runs the Services on its behalf, under this same policy. People in the EU, UK and Switzerland can find more on our GDPR page, and a plain-words summary of our DPDP commitments is on our Data Protection page.

2. Our two roles

  • For our own users (people who sign up for, or visit, Leadschilly), we decide how their data is used. Under the DPDP Act we are the Data Fiduciary.
  • For our customers’ contacts (the leads, buyers and callers that a business manages in Leadschilly), the business decides why and how that data is used. We process it on the business’s instructions as its Data Processor. If you are a lead or buyer of a business that uses Leadschilly, that business is responsible for your data; please contact them first. We will help them respond to you.

3. Data we collect

3.1 Account and business data

Name, email address, phone number, business name, team members and their roles, billing details, and the settings you choose.

3.2 Lead and customer data entered by businesses

Names, phone numbers, email addresses, requirements (for example budget, location, property type), notes, tags, appointments, tasks, messages, and payment milestones that a business adds or imports.

3.3 Data from connected services

  • Facebook and Instagram (Meta) lead ads: when a business connects its Facebook Page, we receive the answers people give in that business’s lead forms (such as name, phone, email and form questions), the time of the submission, and identifiers of the form, campaign, ad set and ad. We receive the list of Pages the business admin chooses to connect.
  • WhatsApp Business: messages exchanged between a business and its contacts through the business’s WhatsApp Business number, message templates, and delivery and read status.
  • Cloud telephony and SMS providers chosen by the business: call details (numbers, time, duration, direction, outcome), call recording links, SMS content and delivery status.
  • Property portals, website forms, and other apps the business connects (for example through webhooks or API keys).

3.4 Leadschilly mobile app

The Leadschilly Android app is being rolled out. Where a business uses it, its sales team installs it on their work phones. With the user’s permission, it reads:

  • Call history (phone number, time, duration and call type) so calls with the business’s contacts are logged against the right contact. Users can choose to sync only numbers that exist in Leadschilly.
  • Call recordings saved by the phone’s own call recorder, only if the user turns this on and selects the folder. The app does not record calls itself.
  • Location, only at the moment a user taps “check in” at a site visit.
  • Device information (model, operating system version, app version) for support and security.

These permissions can be turned off at any time in the app or in the phone’s settings.

3.5 People who book through our booking pages or fill our forms

Name, phone number, email (optional), the time slot chosen and any answers given. This is passed to the business whose page it is.

3.6 Website visitors

Device and browser information, IP address, pages visited, and cookies needed to keep you signed in. We do not use advertising cookies on the application.

On our marketing website leadschilly.com we use Google Analytics to count visits and which buttons are used (for example “Send Hi on WhatsApp” or “Book a demo”). Google Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. For visitors in the EEA, UK and Switzerland, analytics cookies are switched off by default. When you message us on WhatsApp or book a demo from the website, your name, number and messages are handled as a lead in Leadschilly, the same way our customers’ leads are.

4. How we use data

  • To provide the Services: deliver leads to the right business and team member, send the messages and reminders a business sets up, log calls, book appointments, and show reports.
  • To run automations and AI features the business switches on, such as instant replies, summarising calls and chats, filling in lead details and scoring interest.
  • To secure the Services, prevent abuse and spam, and keep an audit log of changes.
  • To provide support, send service notices and bills.
  • To improve the Services using aggregated, de-identified usage information.

We do not sell personal data. We do not use our customers’ lead data to advertise to those leads ourselves. We do not use it for any other business of ours, including any real estate advisory or brokerage business in our group.

5. AI processing

Some features use artificial intelligence providers to reply to leads, summarise calls and conversations, and suggest replies. Only the data needed for the task is sent. Under our contracts with them, our AI providers may not use our customers’ data to train their models, and every AI action is logged so a business can see what was done and why. Businesses can choose whether AI agents only draft, ask before sending, or act automatically.

6. Who we share data with

We share personal data only with service providers that help us run the Services, under contracts that require them to protect it, and only as needed:

  • Supabase (database and hosting, Mumbai region, India)
  • Cloudflare (website and application delivery, security, through its global network)
  • Google (Google Analytics on our marketing website)
  • Meta Platforms (Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp, when a business connects them)
  • Telephony, SMS and email providers that a business chooses and connects
  • Anthropic (Claude), our AI provider, which processes data in the United States, when a business switches on the AI features in section 5
  • Payment processors such as Razorpay (for billing)
  • Zoho (our company email, and Zoho ZeptoMail for the emails the app sends)

We may also disclose data when required by law, a court order or a government authority, or to protect the rights and safety of our users and the public. If Leadschilly is merged or acquired, data may transfer to the new owner under this policy.

7. Data from Meta (Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp)

  • We use data received from Meta only to provide the Services to the business that connected its account: delivering its leads and messages and showing its campaign results.
  • When a business connects its Meta dataset, we send Meta the lead events that business chooses (for example qualified, site visit booked, booked or lost), so the business can measure and improve its own ads.
  • We do not sell Meta data, use it for our own advertising, or share it with data brokers.
  • When a business disconnects its Facebook Page or WhatsApp number, we stop receiving new data from Meta. A business can delete the data already received at any time, and it is deleted when the account is closed (see section 10).

8. Call recordings and messages

Businesses that record calls or send messages through Leadschilly are responsible for informing their contacts and for following the laws that apply to them, including telecom (TRAI/DLT) rules for SMS and WhatsApp’s policies. We recommend a short announcement at the start of recorded calls.

9. Where data is stored

Our main database is hosted in India (Mumbai). Some providers listed in section 6 process data outside India, including Anthropic in the United States for the AI features, Cloudflare’s global network and Google. When they do, we rely on their contractual commitments to protect it, and we follow any restrictions the Government of India sets under the DPDP Act.

10. How long we keep data

  • Account and business data: while the account is active, and up to 90 days after it is closed, after which it is deleted.
  • Items a business deletes: kept in its recycle bin for 30 days, then permanently deleted.
  • Call recordings and messages: for as long as the business keeps them, or the retention period it sets.
  • Records we must keep by law (for example invoices): for the period the law requires.
  • Security, access and audit logs: kept securely in India for at least 180 days, as CERT-In’s directions require, and from 13 May 2027 for at least one year where the DPDP Rules require, with restricted access, then deleted.
  • From 13 May 2027, some personal data may also be kept for one year from when it was processed, only for the limited purposes the DPDP Rules allow (such as lawful requests from authorities), and then deleted.
  • Backups: deleted data may stay in encrypted backups for a short time until the backups are overwritten.

11. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each business’s data is kept separate by access rules enforced in the database, so one business cannot see another’s data. Access within a business follows the roles it sets (owner, manager, sales rep, channel partner). Secrets such as API keys are stored so that they can’t be read back in the app. Every change is recorded in an audit log. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your personal data, we will report it to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing it, as Indian law requires today, and inform you, the business concerned and, from 13 May 2027, the Data Protection Board of India, as the law requires.

12. Your rights

Under the DPDP Act you can:

  • ask for a summary of the personal data we process about you and how we process it;
  • ask us to correct, complete or update it;
  • ask us to erase it, where we no longer need it or you withdraw consent;
  • withdraw consent you have given, as easily as you gave it;
  • nominate another person to exercise these rights if you die or are unable to; and
  • raise a grievance with us, and then with the Data Protection Board of India if you are not satisfied.

To use these rights, email admin@leadschilly.com from the email or phone linked to your data. If your data is held by a business that uses Leadschilly, we will forward your request to that business.

13. How to delete your data

  • Leadschilly users: the account owner can delete leads, team members and the whole account from Settings, or email admin@leadschilly.com with the subject “Delete my data”.
  • Leads and buyers of a business: ask that business, or email us with your phone number and the business name; we will pass it on and confirm when it is done.
  • If you connected Facebook: in Facebook go to Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (or Apps and websites), and remove Leadschilly. Then email us to delete the data already received.

We complete deletion requests within 30 days and confirm by email. Records the law requires us to keep are held separately until their period ends (see section 10). More details are on our data deletion page.

14. Children

The Services are meant for businesses and are not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data of anyone under 18. If you believe a child’s data has been shared with us, contact us and we will delete it.
