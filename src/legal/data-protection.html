Data Protection under India’s DPDP Act

Last updated: 25 October 2026

India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (“DPDP Act”) and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025 set out how personal data must be handled. The Rules were notified on 13 November 2025 and come into force in phases, with most duties applying from 13 May 2027. We are preparing for these duties ahead of May 2027. Until then, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and its rules, including the 2011 rules on sensitive personal data and CERT-In’s directions on cyber incidents, already apply to us, and we follow them. This page describes our current practices in plain words. The full detail is in our Privacy Policy.

1. Our role

  • For people who sign up for or visit Leadschilly, Leadschilly Technologies Private Limited decides how their data is used. We are the Data Fiduciary.
  • For the leads and buyers a business manages in Leadschilly, that business is the Data Fiduciary. We process their data only on the business’s instructions, as its Data Processor.

2. Notice and consent

  • We tell people what data we collect, why, and how to use their rights, in our Privacy Policy and at the point of collection.
  • Where we rely on consent, it is specific, freely given and as easy to withdraw as to give. Withdrawing consent stops future processing; it doesn’t undo processing already done lawfully.
  • Our booking pages and forms show the business’s name, so people know who they are sharing their details with.

3. Your rights

You can ask us to:

  • give you a summary of the personal data we process about you, how we process it, and who we have shared it with;
  • correct, complete or update it;
  • erase it, when it’s no longer needed or you withdraw consent;
  • let a person you nominate exercise these rights if you die or can’t act yourself; and
  • resolve a grievance about how we handled your data.

How: email admin@leadschilly.com from the email address or phone number linked to your data, with the subject “My data”. We may ask you to confirm it’s you. If your data belongs to a business that uses Leadschilly, we pass your request to that business and help it respond. You can also use our data deletion page.

4. Timelines

  • We acknowledge every request and grievance within 24 hours.
  • We resolve grievances within 15 days and complete other requests within 30 days, and always within the time limits the law sets.
  • If you’re not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

5. Keeping data safe

  • Data is encrypted in transit, and each business’s data is kept separate by access rules in the database.
  • Access inside a business follows the roles it sets (owner, manager, sales rep, channel partner).
  • We keep audit logs of sign-ins, exports and changes. We keep these logs securely in India for at least 180 days, as CERT-In’s directions require today, and from 13 May 2027 for at least one year, as the DPDP Rules require.
  • If a breach affects personal data, we report it to CERT-In within 6 hours of noticing it, as required today. From 13 May 2027 we also inform the Data Protection Board of India and the people affected, in the manner and within the time the DPDP Rules set.

6. How long we keep data

We keep personal data only as long as we need it for the purpose it was collected, or as the law requires. Account data is deleted within 90 days after an account closes, or within 30 days if the owner asks us to. Items a business deletes stay in its recycle bin for 30 days, then are deleted permanently. Security logs, and records the law requires us to keep, are held separately for the period the law sets. The full list is in section 10 of the Privacy Policy.

7. Children

Leadschilly is a business service and isn’t meant for anyone under 18. We don’t knowingly process children’s data. If you believe a child’s data has reached us, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data outside India

Our main database is in India (Mumbai). Some service providers listed in the Privacy Policy, including our AI provider, process data outside India. We use them under contracts that protect the data, and we follow any restrictions the Government of India sets on transfers.

9. Help for businesses using Leadschilly

As Data Fiduciaries for their leads, businesses have their own duties. Leadschilly helps with:

  • opt-out handling: people who reply “STOP” or similar are marked Do Not Disturb and are not messaged by automations;
  • deleting a lead’s data, with a 30-day recycle bin before it is gone for good;
  • exporting a lead’s data on request;
  • an audit log of every change and every AI action; and
  • our main database hosted in India (Mumbai).

A data processing agreement is available for businesses that need one: contact admin@leadschilly.com.
