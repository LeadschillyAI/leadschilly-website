Delete your data

You can ask Leadschilly to delete your personal data at any time. We complete requests within 30 days and confirm by email.

If you use Leadschilly (business owners and team members)

  1. Sign in to Leadschilly. The account owner can delete leads, team members and other records from the app. Deleted items stay in the recycle bin for 30 days, then are removed permanently.
  2. To close the whole account and delete all its data, email admin@leadschilly.com from the owner's email address with the subject "Delete my account". All data is deleted within 30 days.

If a business contacted you through Leadschilly

Your details belong to that business, which decides how they are used. You can:

  1. ask the business directly to delete your details, or
  2. email admin@leadschilly.com with the subject "Delete my data", your phone number, and the name of the business. We will pass your request to the business and confirm when it is done.

If you connected your Facebook Page or Instagram account

  1. In Facebook, open Settings & privacy → Settings → Business integrations (on some accounts this is called Apps and websites).
  2. Find Leadschilly and click Remove. We stop receiving new data straight away.
  3. To delete data already received from Facebook, email admin@leadschilly.com with the subject "Delete my Facebook data" and the name of your Page. We delete it within 30 days and confirm by email.
